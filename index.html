Amogh Inamdar

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science, Columbia University

Hello, and welcome to my webpage! I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Columbia University, advised by Dr. Richard Zemel. My research is currently focused on improving the process for training machine learning models. Human learning and machine learning (ML) currently follow pathologically different strategies. While human learning is continuous and curriculum-driven, ML predominantly focuses on repeated presentations of randomly sampled data from large training datasets. The latter paradigm is notoriously far less data efficient than the former, particularly for massive multi-task learners like AI 'foundation models'. Through my research, I aim to improve our understanding of ML models in continual learning settings, and develop more effective and efficient ways to train these models.

Prior to the doctoral program, I graduated from Columbia with a Master's degree in Computer Science and worked on deploying and managing massive-scale Kubernetes clusters as a software engineer on the Data Cloud Platform at Salesforce.
